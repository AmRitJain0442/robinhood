Use both interfaces

Launch robinhood for the terminal or robinhood gui for this interface. From the terminal, /gui opens this view on the same live session. Close the browser tab to leave the engine running; /quit exits the engine. One process owns the session database at a time.

Cumulative totals cover saved session events. Deleting sessions removes their usage records. Robinhood does not claim to know your remaining free balance or billable cost.
